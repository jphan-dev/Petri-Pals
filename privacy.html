Privacy Policy

Petri Pals · Last updated 10 November 2026

Petri Pals does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Nothing you do in the app is sent to me or to anyone else.

What the app stores

Your game progress (your character, your collection, your lab, your settings and your backups) is saved locally on your device using standard web storage inside the app. It never leaves your iPhone. I have no way to see it, retrieve it, or restore it for you.

If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. The app includes an export feature so you can copy your own save code and keep it yourself.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, the app can submit four scores to Apple's leaderboards: species collected, reputation, papers published and money earned. This only happens if you sign in, and it is handled entirely by Apple. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can play the whole game without ever signing in.

Links out of the app

Microbe pages include an optional link to search Wikipedia for further reading. Tapping it opens Wikipedia in a browser view. I receive nothing from that, and Wikipedia's own privacy policy applies there.

Network use

The game itself runs entirely offline. It makes no network requests except the optional Wikipedia link and Game Center, both described above.

Children

Petri Pals is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no chat and no user-generated content shared with others.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
