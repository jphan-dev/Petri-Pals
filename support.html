Support

Petri Pals · Last updated 10 November 2026

Questions, bug reports, species suggestions and corrections are all welcome at jphanmadeapps@gmail.com. I usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no accounts.

Does it need the internet?

No. The whole game runs offline.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. The Lab tab keeps automatic backups you can roll back to, and it can export a save code you can paste onto another device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

I lost my lab. Can you restore it?

Unfortunately not, because nothing is stored on a server. Check the Lab tab for an automatic backup, and keep an exported save code in future.

Is the science accurate?

That is the point of the game. The species, their growth conditions, cell shapes, stain results, biochemical tests and genes are all real. Timings and quantities are compressed so it works as a game. If you spot an error, please email me, as corrections are genuinely appreciated.

Can I use it for teaching?

Yes, please do. If you use it in a class or outreach session, I would love to hear how it went.

Accessibility

The Lab tab has colorblind mode, three text sizes, large touch targets and a plain high-legibility font. The app also follows your system's reduce-motion and dark mode settings. If something is hard to use, tell me and I will fix it.
