Petri Pals

A free, ad-free microbiology game for iPhone. Collect, identify, culture and engineer 81 real microbes, from the bacteria on your own skin to an archaeon from a deep-sea vent.

No ads No tracking No accounts Plays offline

What you do

  • Sample 21 habitats and pick colonies off a plate
  • Identify unknown isolates with Gram stains, biochemical tests and culture tests
  • Grow cultures at the right medium and temperature, and harvest at the right moment
  • Clone real genes between species and check your clones on a gel
  • Run 15 experiments, from phage hunting to lab evolution
  • Publish papers and work your way up from grad student to professor
